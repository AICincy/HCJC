Methodology

Source: bond amounts printed on the HCSO public inmate roster, mirrored by this site (ORC § 149.43). Each person contributes one amount: the posted bond of their first-listed charge, attributed to that charge's ORC section. People with no parseable bond amount, or a $0 amount, are excluded. This is a snapshot in time of the current roster, not a longitudinal study; it changes with every update cycle.

Statutes with fewer than 5 qualifying people are suppressed entirely, so no row can identify an individual's bond. Quartiles are computed with Python's statistics.quantiles (n=4, exclusive method); spread is Q3/Q1.

Read this page carefully: bond amounts reflect judicial decisions made on individual facts (criminal history, flight risk, specific conduct charged) that are not visible in this data. A wide spread shows dispersion, not misconduct. Charges under the same ORC section can differ by degree and subsection, and everyone listed is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law.
