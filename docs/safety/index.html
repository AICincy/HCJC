Community safety

Cincinnati Open Data, explained in plain English

Beyond the jail roster, the City of Cincinnati publishes public-safety data: 911 calls for service, reported crimes, police stops, and more. This page translates those feeds into plain language. Everything here is reported, not proven: a call, a stop, or a report is not a conviction, and none of it is matched to anyone on the roster.
