Arrest is not conviction. Everyone listed here is legally presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law. Charges are accusations only. Tiers shown are the statutory default for the cited section. The degree actually charged in a case can be heavier or lighter based on subsections, prior offenses, and aggravating factors determined by the charging authority or the court.
