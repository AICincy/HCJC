Help & Self-Help

Free legal aid, court services, and the courts' own guides

JCStream is not legal advice and not a lawyer. This page mirrors current contact information for the free Hamilton County resources most relevant to people who land on this site looking for help with the justice system. Start with the category that matches your situation.

Help resources captured 2026-09-21.

Scam alert. Scammers actively impersonate Hamilton County Sheriff's Office, Electronic Monitoring Unit, and Clerk of Courts personnel, calling families of incarcerated individuals and demanding fees for "release via electronic GPS ankle monitor." They use spoofed numbers and publicly available arrest records, and request payment via Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App. Hamilton County will never require payment for electronic monitoring before release from the Justice Center. Verify directly with the Electronic Monitoring Unit at 513-946-9800.

I Want a Record Sealed or Expunged

Fresh Start Expungement Clinic
Free clinic run by the Public Defender. 1st and 3rd Wednesday of each month, 1:00 PM-4:00 PM. Walk-ins are not accepted. Call 513-946-3666 between 8:30 AM and 11:30 AM on the clinic date you want to attend to claim an appointment, first come first served.
Recent expansion (3/19/2025)
Convictions for Violating a Protection Order are now eligible to be sealed. Convictions for Domestic Violence (M1 or below) and Felony Failure to Appear (F4) are now eligible to be expunged after the applicable waiting period.
If your charge was a referral to prostitution / human trafficking
Special expungement assistance is available through the Public Defender. 513-946-3700.
Why bother
A juvenile or criminal record - even decades old - can affect employment, housing, professional licensing, immigration status, and benefits eligibility.

Appearing in court

Moved from the Courts tab. Mirrored from hamiltoncountycourts.org/appearing.

Arrive early
Plan to arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled court appearance. All persons entering the Courthouse and Justice Center must pass through a metal detector. Between 8:30 AM and 9:30 AM, expect longer lines.
Courtrooms A & B
Located in the Justice Center (the jail), 1000 Sycamore Street, behind the Courthouse. Separate from the Courthouse at 1000 Main Street.
Parking
Expect to pay $5-$10 depending on location. Map and parking lots →
Dress code
Neat, clean, and appropriate. No tank tops, tube tops, shorts above the knees, hats, do-rags, or caps.
Electronics
Cell phones, pagers, cameras, and other electronic devices are prohibited without court permission. Violations are subject to contempt, seizure, or ejection.
Prohibited items
Pocket knives, scissors, mace, or any other weapon. Deputies will not issue claim tickets for confiscated items. Leave them in your car.
Failure to appear
In a criminal case, a bench warrant is typically issued for your arrest. In a civil case, you may lose by default. If you cannot appear, contact your attorney, the bailiff, or the Assignment Commissioner at 513-946-6250.
Find your hearing
Assignment Commissioner: 513-946-6250 (Room 117, Justice Center or Room 255, Courthouse, 8 AM-4 PM). Clerk of Courts 24-hour line: 513-946-6010. Kiosk terminals near courthouse elevators show the next 7 days of scheduled hearings.

Why This Page Exists

Many people land on JCStream because someone they love was just booked or because a record about them is showing up in public search results. The site itself is a public-records mirror and is not equipped to help. The agencies and organizations above are. This page is a curated, single-screen index of the free Hamilton County resources most relevant to people in those situations, written in plain English and kept current.

Phone numbers and links are verified from the agencies' own sites. If you find a number that has changed or an organization that no longer offers a listed service, please open an issue. There is never a fee, and there never will be.
