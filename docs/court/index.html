Area Courts hear some traffic cases on Mondays and Wednesdays at 6:00 PM in Courtroom B of the Justice Center, 1000 Sycamore Street. OVI initial appearances are in Courthouse Room 121, or Courtroom A of the Justice Center if the defendant is incarcerated. Questions: Clerk of Court's office 513-946-6041.
If you cannot appear
Failure to appear can lead to a bench warrant in a criminal case or losing by default in a civil case. Do as many of these as you can, as soon as you can: if you are a witness subpoenaed by the prosecuting attorney, call that office and have your case number ready. Hamilton County has two prosecutors: the City Prosecutor handles Municipal and misdemeanor cases that occurred inside the City of Cincinnati; the County Prosecutor handles cases that occurred outside the city and all Common Pleas felony cases. Case numbers beginning with "C" are handled by the County Prosecutor. If you were subpoenaed by the defense attorney, call that attorney instead. If you are a defendant or party with a lawyer, call your lawyer first. Call the bailiff of the courtroom where you are scheduled to appear and explain why you cannot. Unless your lawyer tells you otherwise, send a relative or a trusted friend to appear and explain. An arrest warrant may still be issued even if you call. Frequently needed phone numbers (PDF) →
First District Court of Appeals
Reviews appeals from Common Pleas, Municipal, and Juvenile Courts in Hamilton County. Decisions released Wednesdays and Fridays. 12th floor, William Howard Taft Law Center.
Hearings are public
Public hearings are open. The court schedule, case files, and filed documents are at courtclerk.org. Pavan Parikh, Clerk of Courts.
Scam alert: electronic monitoring "release fees" Scammers are actively impersonating Hamilton County personnel, demanding fees to release an incarcerated person to electronic GPS monitoring. Hamilton County will never require payment for electronic monitoring before release. Verify directly with the Electronic Monitoring Unit at 513-946-9800.

Need a public defender
If you cannot afford an attorney, the court will appoint one. The Hamilton County Public Defender handles adult and juvenile criminal cases. Adult: 513-946-3700. Juvenile: 513-946-8282.
Civil matters, no lawyer
The Clerk of Courts Help Center at Room 113, Hamilton County Courthouse, provides free information, document help, and free 40-minute attorney consultations for civil matters. 513-946-5650. Mon-Thu 8 AM-3:30 PM, Fri 8 AM-3 PM.
