Visiting Someone at the Justice Center

Hamilton County Justice Center · 1000 Sycamore Street, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202

JCStream does not handle visitation. This page reproduces the published Hamilton County Sheriff's Office (HCSO) policy and contact information so family members who arrive here looking for the actual policy don't have to hunt for it. The HCSO page is the source of record →

Scam alert: electronic monitoring "release fees" Hamilton County will never call you, or your incarcerated loved one's family, demanding a fee for an electronic GPS ankle monitor before release. The Hamilton County Court of Common Pleas has issued a public warning that scammers impersonate the Sheriff's Office, the Electronic Monitoring Unit (EMU), and the Clerk of Courts. They use publicly available arrest records and demand non-traceable payments via Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App. If you receive one of these calls, hang up and verify with the EMU directly at 513-946-9800.

Rules HCSO Enforces

  • The registered visitor must be present at the time of the visit.
  • Visitors must be appropriately dressed for the entirety of the visit. Absolutely no nudity of any kind.
  • No illicit or lewd behavior. No weapons or dangerous ordnance displayed during the visit.
  • Individuals must be more than 30 days from their release date to be visitable.
  • No active temporary protection orders (TPOs) against the inmate naming the visitor.
  • No active warrants out for the visitor.
  • Valid government-issued photo ID is required to register and to visit.
  • No minors without an adult present. Maximum four visitors at one time.
  • The account must include a photo of the visitor's government ID, date of birth, ID number, and expiration date.
  • Profile pictures cannot contain any social-media filters.
  • Violation of any rule results in immediate termination of the visit without credit and a potential ban from all future visits.

Inmate Accounting & Commissary

Inmate Accounting
Room 120, Justice Center North Building. 513-946-6630. Mon-Fri 8 AM to 4 PM, closed weekends and holidays.
Deposits
Via kiosks in the lobby of the North and South buildings, or online at commissarydeposit.com. Cash cannot be handed to jail staff.
Commissary spending limit
$75.00 per order. Commissary slips are collected at 4 AM two days before delivery; funds must be on the account by then. If order exceeds balance, hygiene items are filled first. Out-of-stock items are not substituted.
Commissary pricing
Commissary Prices (PDF, Jan 2026) →
Release pay
Upon release, remaining account balance is placed on a Release Pay Debit Card. Cardholder support: 877-287-2448. releasepay.com.
Releasing funds while incarcerated
One-time emergency release allowed (cannot exceed amount entered with). Request form from Social Services. Funds picked up at Room 120, valid government ID required. Inmate Accounting contacts the designee once approved.

Inmate Property

Property window
South Building, Justice Center. 513-946-6330. Mon-Fri 8 AM to 4 PM, closed holidays.
Releasing property
Inmate fills out a release form via the kiosk on their floor. Valid government ID required for pickup. Property = clothing, wallets, keys. Money is released separately through Social Services.
Dropping off items
Prescription glasses, contact lenses (sealed/unopened box only, no solution), and dentures can be delivered Mon-Fri 8 AM to 3:30 PM. Valid photo ID required; all items searched.
Court clothing drop-off
Accepted 8 AM to 10 AM the day before court (Friday if court is Monday). Valid ID required. All clothing searched by drug dog. Acceptable: polo, khakis, button-up shirt, suit. No belts, ties, suspenders, or shoes. Clothing sprayed with cologne, perfume, or scented oil will not be accepted.
Shipping property after release
Items left behind can be shipped at receiving person's expense. Send a prepaid box to: Hamilton County Justice Center, 1000 Sycamore Street, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202, Attention: Property Department.

Posting Bond

Bonds are posted in person, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, at the Clerk of Courts Municipal Criminal/Traffic Division on the 1st Floor of the Hamilton County Justice Center, 1000 Sycamore Street.

For the court's published standard bond amounts, see the Standard Bond Schedule.

What JCStream Will Never Publish

By policy, even when the underlying data may technically be public. See data & legal for the full scope statement.

  • Identities, addresses, or phone numbers of visitors.
  • Phone-call records, transcripts, or recordings.
  • Commissary purchases, deposit amounts, or money transfers.
  • Internal classification (gang affiliation, security level, behavior notes).
  • Medical, psychiatric, or program-participation information.
  • Identifying information about minors, victims, or witnesses.

If a Record on This Site Is Causing You Harm

Records here come and go automatically: when HCSO removes a person from the public roster, that record drops from JCStream on the next sweep (hourly, best-effort). For earlier removal, for example after a court order of sealing or expungement under ORC § 2953.32, as amended, open an issue at github.com/AICincy/HCJC/issues.

There is never a fee, and there never will be.
