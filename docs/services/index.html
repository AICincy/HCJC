Specialized Dockets (Common Pleas) hamiltoncountycourts.org

Specialized dockets are particular sessions of court or dockets that offer a therapeutically oriented judicial approach to providing court supervision and appropriate treatment to individuals.

Specialized Dockets (Municipal) hamiltoncountycourts.org

The objective of Veterans Court is to divert veterans from the traditional criminal justice system to a treatment-based court in order to rehabilitate and assist veterans in leading a productive and law abiding life.
