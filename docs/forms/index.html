Court Forms

Common Pleas, current generation rev. 4-24-26

The forms the court currently accepts, plus prior generations and related forms. Always file the current generation.

Court forms captured 2026-09-21.

Source links verified 2026-09-22. All 49 forms below link to the court's own files, each checked live on 2026-09-22 after the court reorganized its uploads. Revision badges (for example Rev. 4.24.26) name the generation the court accepted when captured on 2026-09-21; that is not a live check against the courthouse, so verify with the court before filing.

What do you need to file?

Pleading guilty or no contest in Common Pleas: use the current plea forms.

Giving up a jury trial: the current Waiver of Trial by Jury.

Cannot afford court costs: the current Affidavit of Indigency.

Filing a discovery plan: see Discovery below.

Scheduling a case or applying to mediate: see Scheduling and mediation below.

You need protection from someone: Ohio courts can issue a civil protection order. That form is not listed on this page yet (see Known gaps); free help resources can point you to the right court.

Looking for an older version of a plea form: see Archived generations.

Known gaps

what the corpus does not have

No Common Pleas forms landing page exists in the court's published corpus.

Protection-order forms (civil, criminal, and stalking protection orders) and record-sealing or expungement forms are not in this corpus.

Municipal forms are two outbound links: courtclerk.org and the Transcript Request Form above.
