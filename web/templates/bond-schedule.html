Standard Bond Schedule

Hamilton County Courts · rev. 3/5/2026

This is the court's published standard bond schedule: the starting point for misdemeanor and traffic bonds in Hamilton County. The judge sets the actual bond at arraignment based on the facts of the case. This page is a reference, not legal advice.

How to read this table

  • $5,000 @ 10% @ 10%: post 10% of the listed amount to be released. The rule is stated as published; the site does not compute the amount you would pay.
  • O.R. O.R.: released on your own recognizance, no money posted, with a promise to appear.
  • NO BOND NO BOND: no standard bond is available for this charge at booking.
  • $5,000 + REVIEW + REVIEW: the listed amount applies but a judge must review the bond before release.

Methodology

This page mirrors the court's Standard Bond Schedule (rev. 3/5/2026) as a structured table so it can be searched and read on a phone. Amounts are shown exactly as published, including the “@ 10%”, “O.R.”, “NO BOND”, and “+ REVIEW” flags. The site does not compute “you post $X” amounts: whether the 10% rule applies unconditionally is not established from the schedule alone.

How do actual bonds compare? See the Bond Disparity Index, which shows how bonds set on the current roster vary for the same statute. The schedule is what the court publishes; the disparity index is what the roster shows. They answer different questions.

For posting bond in person, see Posting Bond. For how a case moves through the courts, see How a Case Moves Through the System.
