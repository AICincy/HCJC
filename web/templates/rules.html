About this page

Methodology: Rules 7, 9, 14, and 29 were reclassified to archival on 2026-09-21 after text comparison verified each was absorbed into the 4-24-26 rules book (similarities 0.995 to 0.999; the book's Rule 14 section is stamped Effective 12-11-2025). The true post-book amendment layer is Rules 31, 17, and 18. Rule 17's effective date comes from the source filename; the document text states only "Rev. 8/2026". Rule 18's effective date is not stated in the document.

See also: court forms, and the bond schedule (rules 14.1 and 14.2 cover bond and bond-forfeiture judgments in the book).
