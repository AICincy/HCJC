Jury Duty

Hamilton County Courts, Jury Commission

You received a jury summons from Hamilton County. This page explains what to do: how to check whether you must report, how to respond to the summons or ask to be excused, and how to reach the Jury Commission. The court's reporting instructions change daily, so always check the live court page for today's instructions, never a cached copy.

You got a jury summons. Three steps.

1. Find your group number on your summons: about an inch below the barcode, the word GROUP followed by a 3-digit number.

2. Check the court's live reporting page the evening before your date, or call the Juror Hotline at 513-946-5879, to learn whether your group must report.

3. If you cannot serve or need a different date, respond by email with a scanned attachment to juryresponse@cms.hamilton-co.org, or through the Juror Web Portal. Grand jurors report on the date and time listed on the summons.

Scam details

The Jury Commission warns that to be called for jury duty in Hamilton County you must first receive a summons in the mail. If you receive a phone call, email, visit, or any other suspicious correspondence from anyone claiming to represent the Jury Commission Office, Sheriff's Office, Clerk of Court, or any other court personnel, contact the Jury Commission at 513-946-5879 to verify any potential jury duty obligations. A newer version of the scam offers the caller the last 4 digits of your Social Security number; the Jury Commission Office will never ask you for, collect, or use your Social Security number.

Archived Jury duty phone scam press release, September 11, 2023

Court of Common Pleas press release dated September 11, 2023; kept for reference.

The courts warned of a resurgence of a long-running phone scam: fraudsters posing as county officials threatened individuals with fines for allegedly missing jury duty and pressured victims into paying by phone with a Green Dot card or similar reloadable prepaid debit cards. One recent victim was told he owed a $400 fine for allegedly missing jury duty and paid before verifying. Administrative Judge Christopher Wagner advised residents to hang up and call the Jury Commission Office at (513) 946-5879. Full release: JuryScam_PressRelease_Final_9.11.pdf (PDF, hamiltoncountycourts.org).

Excuses and postponements

Any request to be excused or postponed must include your 6 or 7 digit juror number, located below the bar code on your original summons. The Jury Commission will send a postcard granting or denying your request; if you have not received one by the Thursday before your summons date, contact the office at 513-946-5879 (press 0 to skip the automated message).

Mandatory excuses

  • Age: if you are over the age of 75 and wish to be excused, the law requires the court to excuse you. Check the box and return the summons with verification of your age.
  • Served in the last 2 years: if you have served on jury duty in Hamilton County within the last two years and wish to be excused, the request will be granted after the office verifies your last date of service.
  • Out of county: if you are no longer a resident of Hamilton County you are ineligible to serve. Reply with verification of your out-of-county address (ID card or driver's license, utility bill, or rental/purchase agreement).

Optional excuses

  • Employment issues: if your employer will not compensate you, submit a letter of verification on company letterhead stating that policy. If you are self-employed, write a letter on company letterhead stating the hardship (you may also submit a business card or recent tax filings; redact SSN/EIN).
  • Student: submit a document verifying current enrollment (class schedule or a letter from the school; do not submit the school's calendar of events).
  • Medical hardship: submit a letter from a medical professional stating you cannot serve; for chronic conditions ask that it be noted. If you are a care provider for an elderly parent or child, submit a letter from their doctor identifying them as needing your consistent care.
  • Prior felonies: jurors with prior felony convictions may serve if no longer incarcerated or on probation/community control. A request to be excused on this ground must include verification of the conviction.
  • Military service, stay-at-home parents, and mothers of a newborn or nursing mothers: contact the Jury Commission Office by phone during the indicated hours to inquire; active-duty military should call rather than send orders in writing.

Postponements

Ohio law allows a one-time postponement: indicate on the summons the Monday you would prefer to start, within six months of your original date. A second request must include a letter explaining why you need additional time.

Work statements and check re-issue
Work statements
Available on request, or print your own from the Juror Web Portal after your service begins. To request: name, 7-digit juror number, and preferred method of receipt (mail, email, or fax). Email bseitz@cms.hamilton-co.org.
Re-issue a check
If your juror check was lost or never received, email the office; uncashed checks are investigated before re-issue, a process that takes 2-4 weeks. Include: status of the old check (lost, not received, stale), juror name, current address, dates of service, and a copy of the old check if stale. Email bseitz@cms.hamilton-co.org.
